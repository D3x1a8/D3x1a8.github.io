Common questions

  • I changed phones. Where is my progress? Progress is saved to your iCloud account. Sign in to the same Apple Account on the new phone, install the game and open it on the home screen; levels, stars, coins and boosters come back within a minute or so.
  • I bought Remove Ads but see ads again. Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases, or reinstall the game while signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • A purchase did not arrive. Close and reopen the game; the App Store finishes paid orders on the next launch. If it still is not there, email us with the date of the purchase.
  • How do I change my privacy or ad choices? iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Settings › Privacy choices inside the game.
