Privacy Policy

Slice Sort and Tumble Jar by D3x1a8 Games · Effective 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information our games Slice Sort and Tumble Jar (the "games") handle, why, and the choices you have. The games do not ask you to create an account, and we do not run our own servers that collect information about you.

Information the games handle

  • Game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, settings) is saved on your device. On iPhone and iPad it is also stored in your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it comes back if you reinstall or use another device with the same Apple Account. We cannot see or access your iCloud data.
  • Advertising. The games are free and show ads through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource) and Unity Ads. To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, these providers may collect device information such as the device model, operating system, language, IP address, app version, ad interactions and device identifiers: the identifier for vendors (IDFV) and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA). They act under their own privacy policies: Unity game player and app user privacy policy (covering Unity Ads and LevelPlay).
  • Consent choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the games ask for your consent before personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. Your choice is stored on your device as an IAB TCF consent string, which the ad providers read. Google's handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive confirmation that a purchase happened, never your payment details.

Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS). The games ask once whether they may track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you do not allow tracking, ads are not personalised with your advertising identifier.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland). You can review or withdraw your consent in the game under Settings › Privacy choices.
  • Progress. Settings › Reset progress deletes your saved progress on the device and in iCloud. Deleting the game removes local data; iCloud data can be removed in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Since we do not hold personal data ourselves, requests about ad data are best made to the ad providers above; we will help if you contact us.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ad providers are told that the games are not child-directed.

Changes

If the games start using another service that handles personal data (for example analytics), we will update this page and its effective date before that change ships.
